# Stage 3A: training sufficiency and baseline fairness (v1, 2026-10-10)

This development protocol is written before new Stage 3A prediction-window scores. It is motivated by already observed Stage 2 results and is not a preregistration or a final-test freeze. Stage 2 is preserved in `preservation/stage2_before_stage3a_20261010/manifest.json` and independent ZIP copies. New code, configurations and outputs use `stage3a_20261010`; old scientific code and results are read-only.

## Boundaries and estimand

The target remains treated effluent daily volume (m³/day), at origin+1 or origin+3 **for that day**. Existing facilities with limited modeling history are simulated, not new-plant start-up. Retain the 10 original development SiteIDs, four 60-origin episodes in 2022–2023, B=30/90/180, and three seeds 20261009/10/11. Source SiteIDs remain 13006/13008/13012, with training up to 2021. No 2024–2025 prediction performance and no reserved-facility data enter any decision. Physical identities remain unverified; use SiteID exploratory terminology. Published Good/nonnegative/nonconflicting QA is used retrospectively for inputs and labels; Missing zeros and negative Good records are masked. Real-time QA availability is unverified. No future interpolation or target imputation.

## Finite diagnostic design

Preserve all eight Stage 2 methods as frozen comparators. Add four Ridge policies: (1) local original 42 columns with tuned alpha, (2) local compact lag-volume features with tuned alpha, (3) shared compact Ridge, (4) shared compact Ridge plus budget-local mean-residual bias. Compact features are normalized volumes at origin, origin−1 and origin−6, with their three availability masks (six columns); no added calendar terms. Median scaling and padding are unchanged; there is no second fitted standardizer. Alpha grid is {0.0001,0.001,0.01,0.1,1,10}. Weights sum to one, so alpha penalizes mean squared error, not an unnormalized sum.

GRU retains the six-channel, hidden=8, 393-parameter Stage 2 backbone; AdamW lr=.01, weight decay=.001, gradient norm clipping=1, batch=256, CPU deterministic one thread per worker. Local and shared trajectories stop at the predeclared cap 300, retaining epochs 40/120/300. Frozen-head trajectories stop at 120, retaining 10/40/120. Candidate selection uses validation MAE averaged over all three seeds; ties select fewer epochs. Training loss remains MSE. We record validation MAE and MSE, explaining their differences, but do not introduce an additional MAE-trained model this round. No prediction-window loss controls stopping. Nonfinite loss fails visibly. Equal epochs and equal updates are both imperfect measures of training adequacy; neither is imposed as a fairness theorem.

## Legal validation and causal initialization

For B90/180, reserve the last ceil(.2B) calendar days of the initial budget. Fit using labels strictly before the validation split; validate at origins from the split through fit_end−h, so every validation label is inside the original budget. Scaling uses the prefix training history only. Require at least 30 fitting labels and 10 validation labels. After selection, refit on the full allowed budget and recompute its median there; parameters remain fixed during each 60-day deployment episode. All lag inputs obey the budget left boundary. Train labels do not cross the validation split.

B30 cannot reliably meet these fold requirements. Its hyperparameters use source-assisted pseudo-deployments on 2021-01-01 and 2021-07-01, with the **same budget**, 14 validation origins and labels within 2021. Each source and pseudo-deployment has equal weight. Any deficient B90/180 fold uses the same declared source-assisted fallback, with the reason reported. These are locally fitted models with source-assisted hyperparameters, not entirely source-independent local methods.

Shared selection fits source 2016–2020 and validates source 2021 with equal-SiteID normalized MAE. Selected settings are refit on source 2016–2021. Local validation choices never use deployment-window targets. Frozen-head sufficiency is isolated using the original 40-epoch source backbone in final predictions: no cross-product of longer pretraining and longer adaptation. For target budget-internal head validation, use a source-2016–2020 40-epoch proxy backbone; a full-2021 backbone would violate chronology for validation origins in late 2021. The proxy/final training-period difference is disclosed. Source-assisted head tuning uses leave-one-source-SiteID-out 2016–2020 pretraining, so the pseudo-target source never enters its own encoder. Source selection records actual dates, label counts, scaling and seeds.

## Evaluation, evidence and stopping

Compare on common finite prediction/truth dates across all selected methods, seeds and budgets; record availability separately. Primary predictions are raw; the secondary constraint is max(0,pred) uniformly for every method, with raw negatives retained. Main statistic is equal-SiteID mean and median MAE skill against Persistence, accompanied by raw-unit MAE/RMSE, wins and negative transfer. Seeds are averaged as per-date losses, not as independent facilities or a prediction ensemble. Matched-structure transfer references are tuned local 42-column Ridge for Stage 2 shared 42-column methods, compact local Ridge for compact shared methods, and selected local GRU for shared/frozen-head GRU. Report both local Ridge feature variants; do not choose the better reference after seeing deployment errors.

Separately compare shared B30 vs local B90/180 and shared B90 vs local B180. These describe history requirements; superiority to a weak model is not engineering adequacy. High-volume dates use the original budget-local 30-day Q90 threshold and are exploratory subgroups, not deployable future-aware selection rules. Report all-date and high-volume performance. Key paired comparisons use synchronous 7/14/28-day moving calendar blocks within episodes and whole-SiteID resampling, 1000 draws, primary block14. Intervals are conditional, pointwise and uncorrected for multiple exploration; unknown physical dependence and only 10 nonrandom SiteIDs limit inference.

Stable evidence beyond Persistence would require positive results at both horizons, support across budgets/seeds/SiteIDs, and intervals robust to block length; this is a diagnostic criterion, not a promised outcome or a certified deployment threshold. Negative and null results are kept. Fixed endpoint scores are training diagnostics and cannot replace the legally selected policies based on development-window ranking.

First check limited tasks for both original smoke SiteIDs, temporal boundaries, data hashes, exact Stage 2 epoch40/head10 endpoints and resume/frozen-head behavior. Then execute all 240 conditions and three seeds with at most four one-thread CPU workers. Save code/data/environment/config hashes, checkpoint states, curves, selections, failures and completion records. GPU and paid resources are unnecessary unless actual measured performance establishes a blocker.

**Stop after Stage 3A.** Residual prediction around Persistence, shrinkage, source-pool expansion, prediction intervals, final testing and software prototypes are proposals only and require review before Stage 3B. Do not claim interval reliability from point-prediction diagnostics.
