## Three priorities and acceptable evidence

1. **Historical grouping and measurement role.** Supply one row for each time-valid SiteID relationship in `FACILITY_GROUPS_TEMPLATE.csv`. A stable, anonymous physical-facility group is acceptable. A stable normal-effluent measurement/outfall group is helpful where facilities share an outfall. Describe linked SiteIDs and the type/effective dates of any alias, meter relocation, merger or shared outlet. If a relationship is unknown, write `unknown`; do not guess from the names. Duplicate a SiteID row when its relation changes historically. Dates may be approximate if the uncertainty and its effect are stated.
2. **Daily data definition.** Complete the volume/date entries in `DATA_DEFINITIONS_AND_AVAILABILITY_TEMPLATE.csv`, or cite one applicable dictionary. Identify m³ per day versus a flow rate, the accumulation interval, date labelling, timezone and DST convention. A written interval rule with its historical validity may substitute for individual daily timestamps. The displayed 86,000-second formula is a documentation question; we have not rescaled released values.
3. **Separate arrival clocks.** In the same template distinguish internal operational observation arrival, initial internal QA, public observation release, public QA release, and final/revised QA. If only a general rule or conservative delay bound exists, specify the dates it covers and its evidential basis. A timestamp of observation or catalogue modification is not proof of first availability. No historical version available is a valid response.

The schema marks **REQUIRED_FOR_A** (independent-facility retrospective claims), **REQUIRED_FOR_B_OPERATIONAL** / **REQUIRED_FOR_B_PUBLIC** (the respective real-time data environment), **REQUIRED_FOR_B** (QA revision evidence relevant to that environment), and **OPTIONAL**. Public-release timing is needed for public-data real-time claims, whereas an internal-operational claim requires its own operational arrival/QA evidence; neither route is presently verified. Required means required to support that claim, not that unknown responses are forbidden or that every item must be answered in the first reply. Retrospective analysis with released/final QA remains possible if explicitly labelled as an idealized information scenario; absent real-time evidence does not invalidate all such analysis. Exact coordinates, catchment polygons, detailed sensor certification and every revision event are **optional or later**, because the present models have no spatial or rainfall inputs.

Evidence strength will be recorded separately: dated official time-valid crosswalk/data dictionary; provider written confirmation with dates; general undated statement; name candidate; unknown. General statements do not certify deployments. Consent to cite/share is requested; restricted metadata can be handled as anonymized group labels if permission permits. No protected prediction errors are required or requested.
