# Supplementary material: SWBiLSTM–GPR dissolved N₂O forecasting

This directory accompanies the manuscript:

**Forecasting Dissolved Nitrous Oxide in Wastewater Treatment with a Hybrid Deep Learning Probabilistic Model**

The study evaluates target-history-based forecasts of dissolved N₂O concentration at a single wastewater treatment plant over horizons of 2, 10, 30, and 60 minutes.
